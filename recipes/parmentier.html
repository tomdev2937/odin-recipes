Hâchis-parmentier

Who doesn't like hâchis-parmentier? Here is a quick way to make it and impress your friends or family!

Ingredients:

  • 800g potatoes
  • 500g minced beef
  • 150g onion
  • 100ml milk
  • 40g butter
  • 50g grated cheese (optional)
  • Salt + black pepper

Steps:

  1. Peel and chop the potatoes. Boil for 15–20 minutes until soft, then drain and mash with the milk, butter, salt and pepper.
  2. Finely chop the onion. Fry it with the minced beef for 8–10 minutes until the beef is browned and cooked through. Season with salt and pepper.
  3. Spread the cooked beef evenly in a baking dish.
  4. Cover with the mashed potatoes and smooth the surface. Add grated cheese if desired.
  5. Bake at 200°C for 20 minutes until golden on top.

And there you have, nice and tasty hâchis-parmentier!
