Carbonara

Who doesn't like carbonara? Here is a quick way to make it and impress your friends or family!

Ingredients:

  • 400g spaghetti (store-bought)
  • 150g guanciale or pancetta
  • 4 egg yolks
  • 1 whole egg
  • 100g Pecorino Romano cheese, grated
  • Salt + black pepper

Steps:

  1. Cut the guanciale or pancetta into small cubes and fry for 5–8 minutes until crispy.
  2. Mix the egg yolks, whole egg, grated Pecorino and black pepper in a bowl.
  3. Cook the spaghetti in salted boiling water according to the packet instructions.
  4. Reserve a little pasta water before draining.
  5. Remove the meat pan from the heat. Add the spaghetti and egg mixture, then stir quickly with a little pasta water until creamy.
  6. Serve immediately with extra grated Pecorino and black pepper.

Tip: Do not add cream. The eggs and cheese create the traditional creamy sauce.

----

And there you have, nice and tasty carbonara!
