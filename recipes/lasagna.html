Lasagna

Who doesn't like Lasagna? Here is a quick way to make it and impress your friends or family!

Ingredients:

For the bolognese sauce

  • 500g minced beef
  • 400g canned chopped tomatoes
  • 150g onion
  • 15ml olive oil
  • Salt, black pepper

For the béchamel sauce

  • 500ml milk
  • 40g butter
  • 40g plain flower
  • Salt, black pepper

For assembly

  • 250g dried lanagna sheets
  • 100g grated cheese

Steps:

  1. Finely chop the onion. Heat the olive oil in a pan and fry the onion and minced beef for 8–10 minutes until browned
  2. Add the chopped tomatoes, salt and pepper. Simmer for 15 minutes.
  3. Make the béchamel: melt the butter in a saucepan, stir in the flour and cook for 1 minute. Gradually whisk in the milk and cook for 5 minutes until thickened. Season with salt and pepper.
  4. Assemble the lasagna in a baking dish: spread a little Bolognese sauce, add lasagna sheets, then béchamel. Repeat the layers, finishing with béchamel and grated cheese
  5. Bake at 180°C for 35–40 minutes, or according to the lasagna sheet instructions, until golden and cooked through
  6. Rest for 5–10 minutes before serving

---

And there you have, nice and tasty lasagnas!
